What does a home cost in a college town?

Median sale prices across 18 towns near 11 university campuses. Each bar and table row shows the middle sale price for that town.

Sales under $10,000 are left out because the data description flags them as implausible. Every median on this page is computed from the remaining completed sales.

Horizontal bars are ordered from lowest to highest median sale price. Values are rounded to whole dollars.
